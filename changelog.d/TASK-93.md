- **A card waiting on your answer speaks up.** A card in _Question_ now raises
  one Herdr popup per grill round — `answer owed — <card>`, the round and its
  title as the body, the `request` sound a merge you owe already uses — instead
  of waiting silently on the board. A new round pops again, a restart does not
  re-pop an announced one, and a paused fleet still announces: the queue waits
  on you whether or not the fleet is scheduling.
