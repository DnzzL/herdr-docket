- **The pane reads at a glance.** Home draws a heading per block, lines its
  card ids up, and explains its row marks (`? question · ! blocked · ⇡ merge
  held`); Round accents question numbers, `(reco)` options and a picked `✓`,
  and explains its footer marks. Three semantic roles from the terminal's own
  sixteen ANSI colours — accent, attention (a stale run, a daemon that is
  down), faint (Settled rows, notes, a disabled Send) — no borders and no new
  glyphs. `NO_COLOR=1` or `TERM=dumb` gives the old plain frame.
