- **A grill round says what it is about before it asks.** The triager opens
  every round with 2–3 lines of context — where the card stands, the files in
  play, the linked card — and `herdr-docket questions` prints them before
  question 1, so you can answer from a phone without opening the card.
- **The pane takes clicks and taps.** Tap a row to select it, tap the selected
  row (or press Enter) to act: one tap on the highlighted question card opens
  its round. The wheel scrolls, and on a phone in Termux a tap is a click.
  Every key the pane had still works.
- **Answer a round with taps.** A question card opens its Round in the pane:
  the context, each option a button with the reco preselected, a plain-words
  field, and Send. `herdr-docket questions` opens it too on a terminal; piped,
  it stays the line prompt.
