- **A confirm round can be sent again.** The triager's numbered assumptions
  were read as questions without options, so the pane's Send never enabled
  and `questions` asked for each in turn. A question is now the line a run of
  lettered options hangs from; the triager lists its assumptions as bullets.
- **A grilled card no longer costs an extra round to confirm.** The triager
  puts its assumptions and "Build on these assumptions once these answers are
  in?" on the round it expects to be the last, so one Send answers and
  confirms. A `roles/triager.md` from an earlier `init` keeps the old rule
  until you replace it with the shipped one.
