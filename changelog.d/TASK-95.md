- **The pane's hints say what to press.** Home wraps its key hint instead of
  cutting it at 45 columns, names ↑↓/1-9 and only the keys that work where you
  are (`p resume` while paused, `x stop` on a run, Enter on a question); the
  Round splits actions from keys, names a letter, ←→/hl, tab and Send, and
  shrinks to one line while you type. Each screen declares its keys once, so a
  key cannot be handled and unnamed or named and dead.
